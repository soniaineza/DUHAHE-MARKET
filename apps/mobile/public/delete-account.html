Last updated: 12 November 2026

Delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Duhahe Rwanda Market app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

What is deleted: your profile, saved addresses, favourites, cart and notifications.
What is kept: past orders are retained for accounting and payment reconciliation, with your name, email and delivery address anonymised.

Cannot access the app?

Email duhaherwandamarket@gmail.com from your registered contact, or message +250 799 654 373 on WhatsApp, with the phone number on your account. We will verify and delete the account within 30 days.

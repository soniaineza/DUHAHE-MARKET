Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Duhahe Rwanda Market ("Duhahe", "we", "us") operates the Duhahe Rwanda Market mobile app and website. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, phone number and a password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Order data: items ordered, delivery province, district and address, optional email, order notes, order status and payment status.
  • Saved data: addresses, favourites, shopping cart and in-app notifications linked to your account.
  • Payment data: when you pay by MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, your phone number and the amount are sent to our payment provider (Paypack) to request the payment on your phone. We never see or store your mobile-money PIN or any card number.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, process and deliver orders, take payments, send order-status notifications inside the app, provide customer support, prevent fraud and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the service: our hosting and database providers, our payment provider (Paypack, for mobile-money payments), and our delivery staff (name, phone and address for your order). We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Past orders are kept for accounting and payment reconciliation; when you delete your account the name, email and delivery address on those orders are anonymised.

Your rights and account deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time in the app: Account → Delete my account. You can also request deletion or a copy of your data via this page or by email. You may also ask us to correct your data.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and sessions expire and can be revoked. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

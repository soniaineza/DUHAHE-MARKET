## 2. Screenshots — how to make them

**What you need**
| Store | Required | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play | 2–8 phone screenshots (aim for 6) | any 16:9–9:16 ratio, 320–3840 px (a normal phone screenshot is fine) |
| Google Play | **Feature graphic** (required) | 1024 × 500 px |
| Google Play | App icon | 512 × 512 px (use `apps/mobile/assets/icon.png`) |
| Apple | 3–10 iPhone screenshots | 6.9" display: **1320 × 2868** (an iPhone 16/17 Pro Max screenshot is exactly this) |
| Apple | iPad | not needed — the app is configured phone-only |

**Which screens (same 6 for both stores)**
1. Home (fresh produce banner)
2. Categories or search results
3. A product page
4. Cart with kilo quantities
5. Checkout with MTN MoMo / Airtel / cash
6. Order tracking (and/or the language switcher)

**Steps**
1. Sign in with a demo account and add a few items so the cart is not empty.
2. On your phone, open the screen and take a screenshot (iPhone: side button + volume up; Android: power + volume down). Screenshot from a real device or an Android emulator / iOS simulator — **not** from a desktop browser.
3. Optional polish (free): put the screenshots in Canva, Figma or "AppMockUp" and add a one-line caption above each phone ("Fresh from Rwandan farms", "Pay with MTN MoMo"). Keep the original app pixels sharp.
4. Feature graphic (1024×500): your logo + the tagline "Fresh Rwandan produce, delivered" on a green background — make it in Canva with the "Google Play feature graphic" template.
5. Upload: App Store Connect → your app → *Distribution* → *iOS App* → *Screenshots*. Google Play Console → *Grow users* → *Store presence* → *Main store listing*.

## 4. Google Play — Data safety form

Play Console → *Policy and programs* → *App content* → *Data safety*. Answer like this:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **Yes** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | **Yes** (HTTPS) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **Yes** — in the app (Account → Delete my account) and at https://duhaherwandamarket.store/delete-account |

**Data types collected** (for each: *collected: yes · shared: no · processed ephemerally: no*)

| Category | Type | Required? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info | **Name** | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info | **Email address** | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info | **Phone number** | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info | **Address** (delivery address) | Required for orders | App functionality |
| Financial info | **Purchase history** (orders) | Required for orders | App functionality |
| App info and performance / Device or other IDs / Location / Photos / Contacts | **Not collected** | — | — |

Notes:
- **Payments:** mobile-money payments are handled by Paypack; the app never sees PINs or card numbers. Do **not** tick "Payment info" (you do not collect card/bank details).
- **Shared:** answer **No** for every type. Passing data to a service provider that processes it for you (hosting, email delivery, payment processing, delivery staff) is not "sharing" under Google's definition.
- **Advertising / analytics:** none. Do not tick them.
